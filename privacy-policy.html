Effective date: 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HIKMA AI Video Services (“HIKMA”, “we”, “us”) handles information connected with this website and enquiries about our creative services.

We may use information you provide to carry out the project you request and may share relevant project materials with production tools or service providers needed to create or deliver the agreed work. We aim to limit sharing to what the project requires. Enquiry and project information is kept only as reasonably needed to respond, manage the work and maintain business records, or as required by applicable law.

You can choose what to include in an enquiry. If you want to ask about, correct or request deletion of information you have sent us, contact hikmadmai@gmail.com. We may need to retain some information where an applicable legal or business record requirement applies.

This website links to WhatsApp and your email application. We do not control external services or their privacy practices. Please review the relevant provider’s information when you use those services.
