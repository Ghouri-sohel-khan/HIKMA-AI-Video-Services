GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

Before we
make a scene.

Want to know what fits your brand? Ask us on WhatsApp and we’ll help shape a direction.

What kind of AI videos can HIKMA create?+

We create AI-led brand stories, explainer videos, avatar-presenter videos, product films, object-talk concepts and social-first edits. The right format depends on your audience, message and channel.

Can the video feature an AI presenter or avatar?+

Yes. We can plan an avatar-led video with a consistent presenter style, script, voice direction and visual setting. Share your preferred language and reference style in your brief.

Can one video be prepared for Instagram and YouTube?+

We can plan versions for vertical and horizontal viewing. The final aspect ratios and deliverables are confirmed with you before production.

How do I start a project?+

Message us on WhatsApp with your business, video idea, audience and preferred platform. We’ll discuss a creative direction and the project scope.

Can I add more videos to the portfolio later?+

Yes. The portfolio currently features the 20 videos you supplied. You can send more clips with a title and category, and we can add them after you confirm they are approved to display.

Can I send my own footage, logo or product images?+

Yes. You can share assets that you own or are authorised to use. Please avoid sending confidential personal or patient information through the website.

Are all the portfolio clips client videos?+

The gallery plays videos supplied by HIKMA. The page does not label them as client projects; contact us if you want details about a specific sample.
